# SD18 Sports — Competitor Analysis

> How SD18 stacks up against competitors in the Indian cricket gear market.

---

## 🏟️ Competitive Landscape Overview

The Indian cricket gear market has a mix of established legacy brands and D2C Shopify stores. Interestingly, several traditional cricket brand domains (DSC, BDM, GM) are **for sale** — suggesting the market is shifting to D2C and platform-based selling.

## 📋 Key Takeaways for Our Pitch

1. **SG is the benchmark** — Professional Shopify, reviews, paid ads. This is where SD18 should aspire to be.
2. **The mid-market is dying** — DSC, BDM, GM domains are for sale. SD18 can fill this gap.
3. **Nobody does community** — Not SG, not Cosco, not CricketerShop. SD18's tournament + academy involvement is a genuine differentiator.
4. **SD18 has 0 paid ads** — While SG runs Google + Facebook ads. This is revenue left on the table.
5. **Everyone uses Shopify + Judge.me** — Industry standard. SD18 is behind on jQuery/Bootstrap template.
