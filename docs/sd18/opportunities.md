## 🏏 Unique Angles (What Makes SD18 Special)

These are things we should **amplify** — they're differentiators:

1. **Bihar State Cricket Academy Sponsorship** — This is HUGE for credibility. Should be front and center on the website with photos, testimonials from academy players, logo placement.

2. **Cricket Tournament Organizer** — Content goldmine. Every tournament = photos, videos, social posts, blog articles, community engagement.

3. **Ground-Level Community Presence** — They're in Asansol physically. Local trust > online-only brands. Leverage this with Google My Business, local SEO, community events.

4. **Budget-Friendly Positioning** — They're not competing with premium brands. They're making sports accessible. This is a powerful narrative.
