## Executive Summary

The cricket equipment category on Amazon India did **₹28.8 Crore GMS** across **5.27 lakh units** shipped in Jan–May 2026 (5 months). SD18's target price range (₹300–₹3,500) accounts for **57% of units and 71% of revenue**. Competition is fragmented — 72% of listings are unbranded, and the median ASIN sells only 3 units total. A well-optimised seller with 30 listings doing 7 orders/day would comfortably land in the top 5% of the entire category.

## 6. Methodology Notes

- **Data source:** Amazon internal category export, cricket equipment vertical
- **Period:** January 2026 – May 2026 (5 months)
- **Columns available:** product_category, product_subcategory, asin, item_name, product_type, manufacturer, brand_name, units_shipped, ops, product_gms, gms, contribution_profit, cogs (all zeros), promo_cost (all zeros)
- **Limitation:** COGS column is empty — this is Amazon's platform view. Seller profitability is estimated separately in the pricing deck.
- **CP interpretation:** Contribution Profit here = Amazon's revenue from the transaction (referral fee + closing fee + shipping markup − fulfillment cost). Higher CP% means Amazon earns more → algorithm favours these products in search rankings.
- **"No brand" caveat:** 14,670 ASINs (84.8%) have no `brand_name` field populated. Some of these may be branded sellers who didn't fill the field correctly. True unbranded share is likely 60-70%, not 84%.
