# SD18 Sports — Website & Brand Audit

> Issues, gaps, and weaknesses found on sd18sports.com. This is our ammunition for the pitch.

---

## 🔴 Critical Issues (Embarrassing — Fix Immediately)

### 1. Typo on About Page — "AD18Sports"
- **URL:** https://sd18sports.com/about-us
- **Found:** `<h3><strong>Welcome to AD18Sports</strong></h3>`
- **Should be:** SD18Sports
- **Impact:** Damages brand credibility immediately

### 2. Placeholder Text in Refund Policy
- **URL:** https://sd18sports.com/cancellation-refund-policy
- **Found:** `Items must be returned within [insert number of days, e.g., 7] days of delivery.`
- **Impact:** Looks unprofessional, legally questionable — a lawyer partner (ours!) would catch this instantly

### 3. Inconsistent Contact Information
- **Main site footer:** `info@sd18sports.com` / `8001818666`
- **Refund policy page:** `sd18sports@gmail.com` / `098008 48482`
- **Impact:** Customer confusion, support messages going to wrong channels

### 4. Leaked Developer Template Link
- **URL:** https://sd18sports.com/about-us (page source)
- **Found:** `<a href="https://ssbcreations.com/demo/frontend/img/docs/SA-770698.pdf" target="blank"></a>`
- **Impact:** Reveals the site is a cheap template, not custom-built. Also an empty link — sloppy cleanup.

## 🟠 Major Issues (Hurts Business)

### 5. No Real Brand Story
- About page uses **generic AI-generated text** — no founder story, no "why SD18", no human element
- They organize cricket tournaments and sponsor Bihar Cricket Academy — none of this is on the website!
- **Opportunity:** Help them tell their real story

### 6. Overloaded Fonts (6 Google Fonts!)
- Loads: Lato, Montserrat, Nunito, Plus Jakarta Sans, Roboto, Cormorant
- **Impact:** Slow page load, inconsistent typography, no clear brand font hierarchy
- **Fix:** Pick 1–2 fonts max

### 7. Outdated Tech Stack
- jQuery 3.5.1 + Bootstrap template
- No modern framework (React, Next.js, etc.)
- No lazy loading, no image optimization visible
- Hosted on Hostinger (budget hosting)
- **Impact:** Slow performance, poor SEO, hard to scale

### 8. No SEO Meta on Inner Pages
- About page: generic/empty meta descriptions
- Category pages: no structured data
- **Impact:** Lost organic search traffic

### 9. Product Images — No Alt Text / Poor Optimization
- Banner images have empty alt tags: `alt=""`
- Product images don't appear to have descriptive alt text
- **Impact:** Accessibility issues, lost image search traffic
