## Problem Statement

Client currently pays ~35% commission to Zomato/Swiggy on every order. For a restaurant doing even 30 orders/day at avg Rs 300, that's Rs 3,150/day or ~Rs 94,500/month going to aggregators.

A direct ordering app eliminates this, keeping the full margin with the restaurant.

## Business Model (Elevate Strategy)

- One-time setup fee (build + deploy + training)
- Optional monthly maintenance/hosting retainer
- Reusable platform for future restaurant clients

## Scope

Phase 1: Customer ordering (app + WhatsApp) + Kitchen dashboard + Delivery staff app
Phase 2: Loyalty/offers engine, analytics, multi-branch support
