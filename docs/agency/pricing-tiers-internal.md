## Pricing Rationale (Internal)

Our cost structure (approximate):
- Website build: 40-60 hours of work over 3-4 weeks
- Monthly SEO: 3-4 hours/month
- Hosting: INR 0 (Vercel free tier, our cost is near zero)
- Maintenance: 1-2 hours/month on average

At these prices our effective hourly rate is INR 1,000-1,500/hr, which is below market median of INR 1,250-1,330/hr for freelancers. This is intentional at this stage to:
1. Build portfolio and case studies
2. Get testimonials and referrals
3. Prove delivery capability
4. Upsell services once value is demonstrated

**When to raise prices:** After 3-5 delivered projects with testimonials, move Tier 2 to 1L and Tier 3 to 1.25L. Monthly retainers can increase 20-30% after 6 months if delivering measurable results.
