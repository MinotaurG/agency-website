# India Digital Services Pricing Reference (2026)

Reference for pricing proposals to Indian SME/mid-size manufacturing clients. Based on market research conducted June 2026 with adversarial verification across 18 sources.

**Exchange rate assumed:** ~84 INR/USD (note: actual rate as of mid-2025 closer to 95 INR/USD, adjust accordingly)

**Key insight:** Marketplace freelancer rates (Guru, PeoplePerHour) are 20-40% lower than what freelancers charge domestic Indian clients directly. Agency rates are 3-5x higher than freelancer marketplace listings.

---

## 1. Custom Premium Website (7+ pages, responsive, CMS, animations, SEO)

One-time cost.

| Provider Type | Range (INR) | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Budget freelancer (marketplace) | 8,800 - 52,500 | Basic responsive WordPress, from PeoplePerHour |
| Mid-tier freelancer | 42,000 - 75,000 | CMS + responsive, moderate customization |
| Experienced freelancer / boutique | 1,00,000 - 2,10,000 | Custom build, animations, headless CMS (Sanity/Strapi) |
| Agency (Clutch-listed) | 83,000 - 4,00,000+ | Minimum project sizes start at 83K |

**Hourly rates:** INR 500-4,200/hr. Median INR 1,250-1,330/hr (~$15-16/hr). Agencies under $25/hr (INR 2,000/hr).

**Hours estimate for 7-page premium site:** 80-150 hours = INR 1,00,000 - 1,90,000 at median rates.

**Confidence:** High (verified 3-0 across PeoplePerHour, Guru.com, Clutch)

## 4. SEO Services (Monthly Retainer)

For B2B/manufacturing keywords (lower competition than retail).

| Provider Type | Range (INR) | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Freelancer (basic) | 2,500 - 25,000/mo | Project-based from Guru.com ($30-$500) |
| Agency (structured) | 21,000 - 85,000/mo | JeewanGarg: starts at $249/mo for 15 keywords |
| Premium agency | 85,000 - 1,17,000/mo | 70 keywords, comprehensive national SEO |

**Key stat:** 76% of India-based SEO providers charge under INR 85,000/month (Ahrefs survey, 439 providers).

**B2B manufacturing advantage:** Low-competition keywords like "potato chips manufacturer India", "bulk snack supplier Jharkhand" are significantly easier (and cheaper) to rank for than retail keywords.

**Confidence:** High (Ahrefs survey + JeewanGarg packages + Clutch data, all verified 3-0)

## Caveats

1. Marketplace rates (Guru, PeoplePerHour) reflect global competition and are typically 20-40% below direct domestic pricing
2. Agencies like BrandLoom and JeewanGarg price in USD targeting export/premium domestic clients
3. No verified data specifically for FMCG/food manufacturing vertical (rates are general, applied to that context)
4. Headless CMS (Sanity/Strapi) commands premium over WordPress but no specific India market data found
5. All data from 2024-2025 marketplace snapshots
