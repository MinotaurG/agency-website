05 Elevate Strategy · Confidential
Website & Brand Issues

What Else Is Holding You Back

Beyond the critical issues, these problems are quietly costing you customers every day.

Design

6 Different Fonts Loaded

Lato, Montserrat, Nunito, Plus Jakarta Sans, Roboto, Cormorant — all on one site. This slows page loading and creates visual chaos. Professional sites use 2 fonts maximum.

Trust

Zero Customer Reviews

No testimonials, no ratings, no Google reviews widget anywhere on the site. Customers buy from brands they trust — and trust is built with social proof.

SEO

No SEO on Inner Pages

Empty or generic meta descriptions on About and category pages. No structured data. No descriptive alt text on images. Lost organic search traffic.

Content

Only 3 Blog Posts

All published Aug 2025, all generic SEO articles. No tournament coverage, no player stories, no behind-the-scenes. No content strategy at all.

UX

Blinking WhatsApp Button

The WhatsApp icon has a blinking animation that feels spammy and unprofessional. Should be subtle and trustworthy, not distracting.

Revenue

No Email or Lead Capture

No newsletter signup, no first-purchase discount popup, no abandoned cart recovery. Every visitor who leaves without buying is gone forever.
