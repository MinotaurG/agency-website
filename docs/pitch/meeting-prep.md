## ❓ ANTICIPATED CROSS-QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

### Pricing & Cost

**Q: "How much will this cost?"**
> "We'll send a detailed proposal after this call with exact numbers. But to give you a sense — this isn't a ₹5-10 lakh agency project. We structure it so you see returns before the investment feels heavy. We want this to be a partnership, not a one-time transaction."

**Q: "Why should we pay you when we can hire a local developer for ₹20,000?"**
> "A local developer can fix the typo. But they can't build an Amazon strategy, write SEO content, draft legal policies, or create ML-powered growth systems. You're not buying a website — you're buying a growth engine. Our team has Amazon, IIM, NLU, and 6 years of content experience behind every decision."

**Q: "Can we just do the Amazon part?"**
> "Absolutely. We can start with Amazon alone and add the website later. But your website issues are costing you credibility right now — and Amazon buyers will Google 'SD18 Sports' and find it. A strong website makes your Amazon store more trustworthy."

### Trust & Credibility

**Q: "You're a new agency. What proof do you have?"**
> "Fair question. Aditya spent 2+ years at Amazon managing vendor portfolios across Europe and tripled signups for finder.io from 300 to 900/month. Adam has 4+ years of legal practice at Ranchi High Court. Our website elevatestrategy.co.in is live — built with the same tech we'd use for you. We'd rather show results than show slides."

**Q: "How do we know you won't just disappear?"**
> "We're from Hazaribagh. We're building this agency as a long-term business, not a freelance gig. You'll have a direct WhatsApp line to both founders. Weekly updates. Monthly reports. And we start with quick wins so you see value in Week 1."

### Amazon Specific

**Q: "Will Amazon sales cannibalize our website sales?"**
> "No — they serve different buyers. Amazon buyers search on Amazon, not Google. They'd never find sd18sports.com. You're not splitting the same pie — you're opening a completely new channel to 279 million monthly visitors."

**Q: "What if we get negative reviews on Amazon?"**
> "That's normal and manageable. We monitor reviews daily, respond professionally, and use feedback to improve listings. Early on, we focus on getting 15-20 genuine reviews quickly through Amazon's 'Request a Review' feature. Your YouTube audience gives you a head start — ask subscribers to review."

**Q: "The fees seem high. ₹96 on a ₹499 cap?"**
> "Think of it this way — you keep ₹403 per cap with zero marketing spend. No rent, no salesman, no showroom. Compare that to your Asansol store where you pay rent, electricity, and staff. Amazon is pure incremental revenue."

**Q: "We already sell offline. Why bother with online?"**
> "Your offline reach is Asansol and nearby. Amazon's reach is all of India — 279 million monthly visitors. Plus, 97,000 YouTube subscribers are watching your content from everywhere. Right now they can't buy from you unless they visit Asansol. Amazon fixes that overnight."

### Technical

**Q: "How long until we see the first sale on Amazon?"**
> "Typically 2-4 weeks from the day we start. Account setup takes 2-3 days, listing takes a week, and then products go live. First sales usually come within days of going live, especially with PPC ads running."

**Q: "Do we need to hire anyone?"**
> "No. We handle everything — setup, listings, PPC, optimization, reports. You just need to pack and ship orders (or let Amazon pick them up with Easy Ship). As volume grows, you might want a dedicated packing person, but that's a good problem to have."

### Competition

**Q: "SG and SS dominate. How can we compete?"**
> "You're not competing with SG on ₹15,000 bats. You're competing in the ₹300-3,500 segment where budget-conscious buyers shop. And you have something SG doesn't — 97K YouTube subscribers, real tournament presence, and a Cricket Academy partnership. That's authentic trust that no ad budget can buy."
