# Pitch — Deck Outline

> Structure for the final presentation to SD18 Sports.

---

## 📊 Presentation Flow (Two Parts)

### Part A: SD18 Audit & Opportunity (The Problem)
*"Here's where you are and what's holding you back"*

| Slide | Content |
|-------|---------|
| 1 | **Title** — "SD18 Sports: Brand Audit & Growth Roadmap" |
| 2 | **Who We Are** — Quick intro to our agency (name, team, what we do) |
| 3 | **What We Found** — Summary: we analyzed your entire online presence |
| 4 | **Critical Issues** — Typos, placeholder text, inconsistent info (with screenshots) |
| 5 | **Brand Gap** — "You say you're a movement, your website says template store" |
| 6 | **Hidden Strengths** — Bihar Cricket Academy sponsorship, tournaments — not visible online! |
| 7 | **Competitor Comparison** — How SG, SS, Kookaburra present themselves vs. SD18 |
| 8 | **The Missed Revenue** — SEO gaps, no email capture, no reviews = lost sales |

### Part B: The Redesign Vision (The Solution)
*"Here's what SD18 could look like"*

| Slide | Content |
|-------|---------|
| 9 | **Before vs. After: Homepage** — Side-by-side mockup |
| 10 | **Before vs. After: About Page** — Real story vs. generic AI text |
| 11 | **Before vs. After: Mobile View** — Current vs. redesigned |
| 12 | **New Page: Community & Sponsorship** — Tournament gallery, Academy partnership |
| 13 | **Social Media Vision** — Content calendar preview, engagement strategy |
| 14 | **Our Agency Website** — Live demo of our site (proof we can execute) |
| 15 | **Proposed Roadmap** — 3-tier plan (Quick Wins → Brand Build → Growth Engine) |
| 16 | **Investment** — Pricing options |
| 17 | **Next Steps** — "Let's start with the quick wins this week" |

---

## 🎯 Key Messages to Land

1. **"We already found 15 issues — imagine what we'll find with full access"**
2. **"You sponsor Bihar Cricket Academy but nobody online knows"**
3. **"Your website was built from a template by SSB Creations — you deserve better"**
4. **"We're not just a web agency — we have a lawyer who can fix your policies too"**
5. **"Here's what your brand looks like when it matches your ambition"** (mockups)

---

## ⚠️ Tone & Approach

- **NOT:** "Your website is terrible"
- **YES:** "Your brand has huge potential that isn't showing up online yet"
- Lead with respect for what they've built on the ground
- Frame everything as opportunity, not criticism
- Let the mockups do the heavy lifting — visual impact > words

---

## 📋 Pre-Presentation Checklist

- [ ] Agency website is live and polished
- [ ] All mockups designed (homepage, about, mobile, community page)
- [ ] Screenshots of all audit issues captured
- [ ] Pricing finalized
- [ ] Proposal document ready to hand over
- [ ] Partner 2 rehearses delivery
