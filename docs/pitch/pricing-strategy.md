## 🎯 MY RECOMMENDATION

### For the meeting: **Present Option B + Option C separately**

**Why:**
1. **Option B (₹3L one-time)** is the "yes" decision — tangible deliverables, clear timeline
2. **Option C (₹75K/month)** starts AFTER launch — they only pay when the foundation is ready
3. Total feels manageable: "₹3 lakh to launch, then ₹75K/month to grow"
4. If they hesitate, drop to **Option A (₹50K)** as a "let's start small and prove it"

### Negotiation levers:
- "First 2 months of retainer at ₹50K instead of ₹75K" (₹50K discount)
- "Include product photography in the launch package" (perceived ₹30-40K value)
- "Start with Amazon only, add website in Month 2" (split the ₹3L commitment)
- "3-month trial on retainer instead of 6" (lower commitment for them)

## 💡 PRICING PSYCHOLOGY TIPS

1. **Never say the total annual number first** — say "₹3 lakh to launch, then ₹75K/month"
2. **Anchor high, then reveal discount** — "Normally this would be ₹4-5 lakh, but because we want to build this as a case study..."
3. **Compare to alternatives** — "A WordPress developer charges ₹50K but gives you another template. A mid-tier agency charges ₹5-8L for what we're offering at ₹3L."
4. **Frame as ROI, not cost** — "You invest ₹3L once. At 5 orders/day, you make that back in 3 months. Everything after that is profit."
5. **Offer payment terms** — 40/40/20 for one-time. Monthly retainer billed on the 1st.

---

## 🚨 WHAT NOT TO DO

- Don't price below ₹2L for the website — it signals "cheap" and attracts "cheap" expectations
- Don't do percentage-of-revenue model yet — too early, SD18 has no Amazon revenue to benchmark
- Don't give indefinite scope — every deliverable must be specific and countable
- Don't offer free work — not even "the first week is free". Everything has value.
- Don't discount more than 20% even if they push — walk away before undervaluing yourself
